Sent only to api.anthropic.com from this page. Kept for this tab; the town game's remembered key is used if there is one.
Each seat is its own call and knows only what that person knows. A whole game is roughly 80 to 150 short calls.

How it plays

  • Knives know each other. Each night they take one person out of the game.
  • Town is everyone else. One of them is the Witness, who learns each night whether one person is a Knife. At eight seats or more one is the Watch, who guards one person each night.
  • Each day the table talks, then votes. Most votes is put out and their role is shown; a tie puts out nobody.
  • Town wins when every Knife is out. The Knives win when they are at least as many as everyone else.
  • Wild cards: the Fool is on nobody's side and wins alone if the table votes them out. The Hothead is Town, and takes one person with them when they go.
  • Whoever is voted out gets their last words before their role is shown. Listen to them.
  • Tap a face to mark them, accuse them, vouch for them, vote for them. Say anything you like in the box: they answer.

The code deals the roles, counts the votes and decides who is out. The people only talk, lie and vote.
