Each seat is its own call and knows only what that person knows. A whole game is roughly 60 to 120 short calls.

How it plays

  • Knives know each other. Each night they take one person out of the game.
  • Town is everyone else. One of them is the Witness, who learns each night whether one person is a Knife. At eight seats or more one is the Watch, who guards one person each night.
  • Each day the table talks, then votes. Most votes is put out and their role is shown; a tie puts out nobody.
  • Town wins when every Knife is out. The Knives win when they are at least as many as everyone else.
  • Tap a face to mark them, accuse them, vouch for them, vote for them. Say anything you like in the box: they answer.

The code deals the roles, counts the votes and decides who is out. The people only talk, lie and vote. Back to the town game.
